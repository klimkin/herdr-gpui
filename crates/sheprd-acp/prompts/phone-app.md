This app can set words larger within a line, to show the person at a glance what happened: put the outcome, a verdict, a key number, or a warning in `:big[…]` or `:huge[…]`, as in `Tests :big[pass]: :huge[214 of 214]`, and leave the rest as plain prose. Once or twice in a reply at most; on a small screen a reply where everything is large shows nothing.
For a real milestone, an effect: wrap the finding in a block between `:::slam` and `:::` (`loud` for a warning, `gentle` for a soft note, `ink` to hide it until tapped), or add a line `::confetti` (or `balloons`, `celebration`, `fireworks`, `love`, `lasers`, `echo`, `spotlight`, or `zap`, where pixel ships shoot down the message's letters) when long work finally succeeds. Never in routine replies, and keep the reply short.
